Most managers describe the work. Here it is on the table.

Two things every owner asks to see and almost never gets shown before signing: the turnover checklist our cleaners run, and what a monthly statement looks like. So here they both are.

Turnover checklist: standard 2BR
Run after every guest, before the next. Cleaner initials & time each line.
  • Strip & launder all linens, inspect for stains, replace if marked
  • Make beds with the second set, hospital corners, count pillows
  • Scrub bathrooms, descale shower glass, fold towel set, restock paper
  • Kitchen: empty fridge, run dishwasher, wipe counters, check every mug is back
  • Restock coffee, filters, soap, salt, paper towels, trash bags
  • Floors: vacuum, mop hard surfaces, check under beds and sofa
  • Test smoke alarms, lights, Wi-Fi, lockbox code, AC set to 72°
  • Photograph the finished home, flag any damage to the owner same day

Short answers to the real questions

Questions owners actually ask.

What exactly is the 25% taken from?

The gross booking income for the month, before channel fees. Cleaning, maintenance, and supplies are separate and pass through at cost. You see every line on the statement.

Do I keep my own Airbnb and VRBO listings?

Yes. The accounts stay in your name. We run them for you, but you own the listing, the reviews, and the history. If you leave, it all goes with you.

Is there a contract or a lock-in?

No long lock-in and no setup fee. We earn your business month to month. If it is not working for you, you give us reasonable notice and we hand everything back tidy.

My home is outside the Carolinas. Can you still help?

Maybe. We are taking on homes in new regions as we grow. Tell us where it is and we will be straight with you about whether we can do it well yet.

How and when do I get paid?

Monthly, with the statement. It shows what came in, what was spent at cost, our 25%, and what landed in your account. One number, no guessing.
