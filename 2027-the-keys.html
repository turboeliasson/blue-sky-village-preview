Short answers

Everything in the “what we handle” list above: hosting, pricing, guest messaging, check-ins, reviews, the lot. Cleaning and maintenance are separate and billed at cost, never marked up.

No. There’s no long-term lock-in and no exit fee. You keep your own listing, so if it isn’t working you can leave and take it with you.

Of course. It’s your home. Block any dates you want for family and friends, and we’ll keep them off the calendar.

Maybe. We started in North and South Carolina and we’re expanding into new regions now. Write to us with where your home is and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can take it on.
