A few honest answers

Questions owners actually ask.

Do I keep control of my home? +

Yes. You stay the owner, the listings stay in your name, and you set the rules: who can book, blackout dates, whether you use it yourself. We run the day-to-day; you keep the say.

What does it really cost me? +

25% of what your home books. Cleaning and maintenance are billed at cost with no markup, and there’s no setup fee. You see one plain statement a month.

Am I locked into a contract? +

No. There’s no long lock-in. Give us reasonable notice and we hand everything back in order: listings, calendars, keys.

I don’t own in the Carolinas. Can you still help? +

Maybe. We started in North and South Carolina and we’re expanding into new regions deliberately. Tell us where your home is. If we can’t look after it well, we’ll say so honestly.

Will you take any house? +

No. If short-term renting isn’t right for your place, or the numbers won’t work for you, we’ll tell you on the first call rather than sign you up anyway.
