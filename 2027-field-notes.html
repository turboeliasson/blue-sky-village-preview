page two · how this goes

Four steps, no surprises

Most owners are running by the end of the first week. Here is the whole thing, start to finish.

  1. 01

    We come see it

    You send the address. We walk the house, take real photos, and tell you honestly what it can book and whether we are a good fit.

  2. 02

    We set it up

    Listing copy, photos, pricing, the calendar, a lockbox or smart lock. We sync it to Airbnb, VRBO and Booking.com.

  3. 03

    Guests arrive

    We handle the messages, the check-ins, the 11pm questions and the turnovers. You get your evenings back.

  4. 04

    You get paid

    Bookings land in your account. We take our 25% and send you a plain statement of what came in and what went out.

the part nobody sees

Everything between bookings

The listing is the easy bit. The work is in the turnover: stripping beds, restocking coffee, finding the one thing the last guest left behind. We do all of it.

  • Cleaning and laundry between every stay
  • Restocking soap, paper, coffee, basics
  • Pricing that moves with the season
  • Photos and listing upkeep
  • Small repairs and a vetted handyman
  • Reviews, and answering the bad ones
  • Inspections after each guest
  • Owner statements you can actually read

quick answers

Before you call

Do you take every home?

No. We only take on homes we can look after properly and that we think will book. If yours is not a fit, we will tell you and point you somewhere better.

Where do you manage?

North and South Carolina today, centered around Mooresville and Lake Norman, and we are opening new regions. If you own elsewhere, call us anyway. We are growing toward good houses.

Is the 25% really all of it?

The 25% is our management fee on what your home books. Cleaning and maintenance are passed through at cost, never marked up. No setup fee, no monthly fee.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. You can leave with 30 days’ notice. You keep your listing and your reviews. We want you to stay because the homes are booking, not because of fine print.

What do you handle, exactly?

Listings across Airbnb, VRBO and Booking.com, pricing, guest messaging, check-ins, cleaning and turnovers, restocking, small repairs, inspections and plain owner statements. The day-to-day is ours.
