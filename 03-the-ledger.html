The short answers.

What does it cost?

25% of gross booking value. Cleaning and maintenance are billed at cost, with no markup and no surprise fees.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. There’s no lock-in, and you keep your listing and reviews. They stay yours.

Can I still use my home?

Of course. Block any dates you want for family and friends. It’s your home.

Who cleans it?

Our trusted local cleaners, every single stay, then inspected before the next guest.

Which channels do you list on?

Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com and more, priced daily to the season.

How soon does it earn?

Most homes are live and earning within about three weeks.
