2 · The principle

The sky is the brand. A company named Blue Sky earns a blue-anchored, daylight-lit identity. The interface stays cool, composed and quiet; all warmth on the page comes from photography, never from gold chrome or beige surfaces. If a screen feels warm, the photo is doing it, and that is correct.

Mist#5E7386 · muted text
Line#D8E2EB · hairlines

Do

  • One accent on the whole page: Sky 600, for CTAs, links, focus and key numbers.
  • Feedback colors only in feedback: success #2E7D5B, error #B4532E.
  • Dark bands run Sky 950, optionally fading to #123049.

Don't

  • No gold, brass or beige chrome anywhere (the old accent #E0B148 is retired).
  • No second accent, no purple, no teal, no decorative gradients.
  • Never pure #000 or #FFF backgrounds.

5 · Imagery: how pictures look and how they are framed

Photography is the only warm element in the system and carries the whole emotional load. Every image must read as a real Carolina lake-country property in believable light.

Art direction

  • Golden-hour or soft morning light; true-to-life color, no HDR.
  • Lived-in and calm: a thrown blanket, an open door, no staged people.
  • Ratio system: hero 3:2 · editorial 4:3 · band 21:9 · detail 4:5.

The frame system

  • Images sit full-bleed (hero, bands) or in 18px-radius frames with a sky-tinted shadow 0 18px 44px rgba(12,31,48,.16).
  • Text over a photo requires a scrim: linear-gradient(to top, rgba(12,31,48,.62), transparent 55%).
  • Hover inside frames: image scales 1.03 over 900ms. Nothing else moves.

Retired image habits

  • No caption chips or pills overlaid on photos (the old blurred dark pill is gone).
  • No gradient "golden hour" placeholder blocks; a slot without a real photo stays an explicit TODO.
  • No paper-grain overlay on top of photography.

Pending assets (generation queued until the Kie.ai balance is topped up): a dawn Blue Ridge 21:9 band, a linens detail 3:4 and a key-on-porch detail 3:4. Slots are marked TODO(gen) in the site source.

6 · Shape, space and components

Radius rule

Interactive elements are pills. Images and cards are 18px. Inputs are 10px. Nothing else.

Space

Container 1180px. Sections breathe at clamp(72px, 10vh, 128px). Grids gap 16 to 48px.

Elevation

Shadows exist only under photography and the form card, always tinted Sky 950, never black.

Primary · pill, Sky 600 Ghost · hairline

One CTA intent per page, one label for it everywhere: "See what your home could earn". The guest link "Book a stay" is navigation, not a CTA. Forms: label above input, Cloud fill, Line border, Sky 600 focus ring, error text below in #B4532E.

7 · Motion

  • Entry: hero content rises 12px and fades, staggered 80ms, once.
  • Scroll: IntersectionObserver reveals only; each element animates once and settles.
  • Hover: CTAs lift 1px; framed photos scale 1.03 over 900ms.
  • Nav turns solid after the hero via an observer sentinel, never a scroll listener.
  • Nothing loops, nothing parallaxes, nothing hijacks scroll. All of it collapses under prefers-reduced-motion.

8 · Voice

Plainspoken, honest, first-person plural, short sentences. The line that defines it: "We'll tell you honestly if your home isn't a fit." Numbers are said simply (25% of gross booking value). Never "elevate", "seamless", "unleash" or any next-gen filler. Norwegian for internal work, English on everything an owner or guest sees.

9 · What this replaces (the v1 audit)

The 2026 "Cover Story" system, retired 26.08.2026

Warm paper cream #F8F4EC with sage #C5D2D0 and a gold accent #E0B148; Fraunces serif display over Hanken Grotesk; a magazine conceit (centered masthead cover, dropcaps, table-of-contents section, pull-quote band); paper-grain overlay at 3.5%; caption chips overlaid on photos; a scroll cue; gradient placeholder blocks standing in for photography.

Why it lost: a brand named Blue Sky had no blue in it; the cream-serif-gold combination is the most generic look in AI-era web design; the magazine framing read decorative where owners need trustworthy; chips and cues added clutter; and the masthead pushed the CTA below the fold on small laptops. What was worth keeping (the honest voice, the heron, the real Lake Norman photography, the proof numbers, the estimate funnel) is all carried into v2.
